Conclusion:
  This newer device still exposes both decoder formats as
  VK_FORMAT_UNDEFINED plus an implementation-defined externalFormat.
  Standards-based opaque import remains usable. The two historical numerical
  mappings also remain consumable in the explicitly enabled diagnostic path,
  but this run does not establish a portable or supported production mapping.
